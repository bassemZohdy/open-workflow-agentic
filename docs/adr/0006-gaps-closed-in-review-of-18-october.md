# ADR-0006: Gaps closed in review of 18 October

**Status:** Proposed (requirement-level, D06)  
**Date:** 2026-10-18

## Context

The 18 October requirements review found gaps in AI inventory, AI incident handling and MCP/A2A consumability.

## Decision

AI inventory added to M18, minimal AI incident handling added to M20, MCP and A2A consumability (native or through a gateway) added to M23; transition and multiple runtimes stay High Priority because S6 tests and M28 already gate their safety.

## Consequences

M18 gains AI inventory, M20 minimal AI incident handling, M23 MCP and A2A consumability. Transition and multi-runtime stay High priority because S6 tests and M28 already gate safety.
