# ADR-0001: Logical Development responsibility

**Status:** Proposed (requirement-level, D01)  
**Date:** 2026-10-18

## Context

The platform has authoring, governance and execution concerns. Treating them as three deployments would force architecture on every solution.

## Decision

Recognize authoring and reproducible lifecycle as logical responsibility, not a new deployment requirement.

## Consequences

Development is a logical responsibility with its own requirements (reproducible lifecycle), not a mandatory deployable.
