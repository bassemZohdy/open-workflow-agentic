# ADR-0005: OSS and OWS priority

**Status:** Proposed (requirement-level, D05)  
**Date:** 2026-10-18

## Context

Open-source licensing and OWS alignment matter strongly, but making them pass/fail would exclude otherwise capable options.

## Decision

Strong High Priority scored dimensions, not automatic mandatory exclusions.

## Consequences

OSS and OWS are scored High-priority dimensions rather than gates.
