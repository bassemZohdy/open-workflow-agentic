**Prior findings**

| Prior finding | Status | Where it is addressed |

| 1. Enforcement mechanism and history exemption | Resolved | Phase 1 selects a `NOT VALID` FK, so existing unallocated rows are retained and new writes are enforced. The inventory is guarded against update, delete and truncate. The plan says not to validate the FK while exemptions remain. Dropping the FK or reopening unallocated writes is a stop condition, not routine rollback. Lock duration is measured in rehearsal and the deployment must fail rather than take an unbounded lock. A9 and A13 test this. |

**Non-blocking notes for the activation PR**

- The `ws1` owner-kind omission is handled by comparing full owner kind and id. The plan could also record it as an accepted limitation, to be fixed only in a new thread version.
- The inventory table is itself an authority-adjacent fact. Its writer, owner and grants should be listed under the A11 role matrix.
- Rehearsal should confirm that the `NOT VALID` FK does not break `UPDATE`s that touch only unrelated columns of exempt rows. It should also confirm the read-only guard rejects updates cleanly. A1 and A9 can carry this.
- The approved-source handoff and the Issue #75/#76 decisions remain hard blockers for activation. The plan states this, so it is not a plan defect.

No material changes are requested.
